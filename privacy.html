Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026

Keepick ("the app") is an iOS application developed by Vladimir Lobanov ("we", "us"). Keepick helps you find and delete similar photos, screenshots, blurry shots, documents and videos in your photo library.

The short version: your photos never leave your iPhone, and we do not collect any personal data.

Photo library access

Keepick asks for access to your photo library solely to analyze and organize it. All analysis (finding similar photos, picking the best shot, detecting screenshots, blurry photos and documents) runs entirely on your device using Apple's on-device frameworks.

  • Photos and videos are never uploaded anywhere.
  • No account or registration is required or even possible.
  • We operate no servers and cannot see your library or any part of it.

Nothing is deleted without your confirmation: photos you trash are moved to the system "Recently Deleted" album, where iOS keeps them for around 30 days.

Data we collect

We do not collect personal information. The app may collect a limited amount of anonymous data:

  • Anonymous usage statistics (via TelemetryDeck): events such as "scan completed" or "purchase completed", together with app version, device model and OS version. This data is anonymized, is not linked to your identity, and contains no photos or photo metadata.
  • Install attribution (via Apple's SKAdNetwork, processed by AppsFlyer): aggregated, privacy-preserving install postbacks provided by Apple. Keepick does not use the ATT tracking prompt and does not access your advertising identifier (IDFA).

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through your App Store account. We never see your payment details. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your device settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions).

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share any user data with third parties, because we do not have any.

Children

Keepick is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
